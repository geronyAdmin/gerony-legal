Gerony

Sports tournaments, live matches and chat in one app.

HomePrivacy PolicyTerms of Service

Gerony is an Android app where people join sports and e-sports tournaments, follow live matches (including live camera and score), chat with friends and teams, and make voice and video calls.

What you can do

  • Join tournaments and follow results and live matches.
  • Chat privately or with your team (text, photos, voice messages).
  • Make voice and video calls to people you chat with.
  • Sign in with your email or with your Google account.
