This policy explains what information the Gerony app ("Gerony", "we") collects, why, and the choices you have. By using Gerony you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account: your email address, display name, username and (optional) profile picture.
  • Sign in with Google: if you choose "Continue with Google", Google gives us your name and email address (and profile picture, if you have one). We never receive your Google password.
  • Content you create: chat messages, photos and voice messages you send, tournament and match activity.
  • Calls: who called whom, when, how long and whether it was answered (call history is deleted automatically after 30 days). The audio and video of a call travel live between the two phones through our real-time provider; we do not record calls.
  • Live matches: if you broadcast a match with your phone camera or microphone, that live video and audio is sent to the people watching.
  • Device information: a push-notification token so we can send you notifications (match alerts, messages, incoming calls).

2. Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera and microphone: only for live matches, calls, photos and voice messages, and only when you use those features.
  • Notifications: for match alerts, messages and incoming calls.
  • Photos / files: only the pictures you choose to send or use as your profile picture.

3. How we use information

To run your account, show tournaments and live matches, deliver messages and calls, send notifications you asked for, keep the service safe (for example blocking abuse), and fix problems. We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Who processes data for us

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and file storage.
  • Google: Sign in with Google and Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications).
  • LiveKit: real-time audio and video for calls and live matches.

These providers process data only to provide their service to us.

5. Who can see your information

Your display name, username and profile picture can be seen by other users. Messages are visible to the people in that chat. You can block people. We do not share your information with others except the providers above or when the law requires it.

6. Keeping and deleting data

We keep your information while your account exists. Call history is removed after 30 days, and uploaded chat media may be removed automatically after some time. To delete your account and data, email us at nagouter@gmail.com from your account email; we will delete it within 30 days.

7. Children

Gerony is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and protected by access rules in our database. No service is 100% secure, so please choose a strong password.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
